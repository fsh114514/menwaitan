# Tokiwa Mako

Tokiwa Mako is a ninja guard of the Takejin family and the latest descendant of the Tokiwa clan that has served it for generations. Bobbed black hair, green eyes, one stubborn cowlick. Teasing little-devil on the outside, innocent girl underneath: she flirts and cracks dirty jokes without blinking, then blushes and wants to run when she is praised sincerely. Deadly with kunai and shuriken, and just as good at cooking and housework.
