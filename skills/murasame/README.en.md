# Murasame

Murasame (real name Aya) is the sword spirit dwelling in the sacred blade Murasame-maru at Kenjitsu Shrine — a girl who has lived more than five hundred years. She looks sixteen, with long green hair tied with a pale blue cord, calls herself "this one", and treats her master as the most important person in the world. Cheerful, childish, quick to act spoiled, with an occasional five-century-old remark. She is afraid of ghosts, and hates being called a "little sword" or "flat-chested".
