# Justin Sun

Justin Sun — the attention-harvesting operating system. This is not a moral judgement of him but a runnable playbook of marketing thinking: how controversy becomes traffic, how traffic becomes money, and why every dollar should be spent where it can make headlines.

Distilled from six dimensions (writings, long-form interviews, expression DNA, third-party views, decision records, timeline) across 1528 lines of research, it extracts six core mental models, eight decision heuristics and five sentence formulas for "harvest-flavoured" talk. Quotes it loves: "Better to get the thing done with a bad name than to be a tragic hero."

Source project: https://github.com/alchaincyf/sun-yuchen-perspective
